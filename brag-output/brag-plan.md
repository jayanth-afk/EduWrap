# Brag Plan: EduWrap (60-Second High-Momentum Entrepreneurship Edition)

## What is this app?
EduWrap is the all-in-one collaborative study workspace for college students and academic cohorts, uniting real-time study rooms with video & audio, multiplayer markdown notes, 3D interactive flashcards with spaced repetition, and an AI-powered quiz generator from course PDFs into a single unified platform.

## The angle
College students suffer from severe "tool fragmentation" — constantly context-switching between Discord, Notion, Quizlet, Zoom, and Google Drive just to study for a single exam. EduWrap solves this by unifying every study primitive into one connected, gamified ecosystem with organic viral growth loops, making it an ideal venture-scale edtech startup.

## Pace & Transitions (Zero-Delay Pacing)
Designed with high kinetic velocity and zero downtime between scenes. Instead of pausing between slides, transitions use swift, seamless cross-glide cuts and continuous camera momentum so the presentation never stalls for a single second.
